# Chilled Water Plant Runbook (Halls A and B)

Last reviewed: 2013-06-02. Owner: Plant Operations.

## Normal operation

Chiller 1 carries the base load. Chiller 2 is staged in automatically when chiller 1 stays heavily loaded.
The sequencer protects the pumps and the loop. Operators should not override it unless instructed.

## Documented parameters

| Parameter | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Rate-of-rise trip threshold | 0.03 °C/s |
| Chiller 2 staging delay | 90 seconds |
| Restart lockout after a trip | 2 minutes |
| Facility power cap | 10500 kW |

## If the cooling loop trips

1. Confirm the trip on the panel.
2. Wait for the restart lockout to expire. The loop restarts on its own.
3. Check chiller 1 and chiller 2 status before resuming normal load.
